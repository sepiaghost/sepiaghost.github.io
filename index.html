about

My work sits at a junction most people treat as two separate careers. Years of hands-on blue-team and SOC work across healthcare and financial systems taught me that the infrastructure carrying biomedical data is inseparable from the reliability of the science built on top of it. My training in genetics and wet-lab biology taught me the other half — what that data actually is. I'm applying that combination to a Ph.D. in bioinformatics and AI for health data: building the computational and machine-learning skills to work directly with genomic and clinical data, and to make that work private and secure, while keeping the systems-and-security lens that got me here in the first place.
